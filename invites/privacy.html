Family Invites

Privacy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Family Invites is a small personal site used to invite friends and family to events and collect RSVPs. It is not a business, it carries no advertising, and it exists only to run these invitations. This page describes exactly what it stores and why.

What this site collects

  • Your name and email address, so an event's host knows who has responded and can contact you about the event.
  • Your RSVP — whether you're attending, how many guests you're bringing (split into adults and children for events that ask for that), and an optional comment you choose to write.
  • Events you create, if you create any — the title, description, date, location, host name, and a photo if you upload one.
  • Basic account details, if you sign in — your email address, the display name on your account, and whether you've set a password. Passwords themselves are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to this site.

What this site does not do

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, and no third-party trackers of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no profiling or automated decisions about you.
  • Your information is never sold, rented, or shared for anyone else's marketing.
  • Nothing is collected beyond what the features above actually require.

Who can see your RSVP

  • The person who created the event, and the site administrator, can see your full response including your email address and comment.
  • Other guests who have RSVP'd to the same event can see your name, your answer, and your guest count. They cannot see your email address or your comment.
  • Nobody else. An event's guest list and its responses are never public, even to someone holding the invite link.

Emails

This site emails you only in direct response to something you did: a confirmation when you RSVP, a notification to the event's host that you responded, and a sign-in link if you request one. There are no newsletters or marketing emails, and there is nothing to unsubscribe from. The messages contain no tracking pixels or remote images.

Where your information is stored

The site runs on Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage, and hosting), so the information above is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure and is subject to Google's handling of it. Confirmation emails are sent through Gmail. These are the only external services involved.

Cookies and browser storage

Signing in stores a session token in your browser so you stay signed in; that is set by Firebase Authentication. If you start an RSVP that needs email confirmation, your answer is held in your browser's local storage until it's submitted, then removed. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

Keeping or removing your information

RSVPs are kept while the event is relevant and are not deleted on a fixed schedule. If you'd like your RSVP, your account, or any other information about you removed, email yaduonline@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

Children

Guest counts may include children attending an event, but that is a number recorded by the adult who RSVPs — the site is not intended for use by children and does not knowingly collect information directly from them.

Contact

Questions about any of this: yaduonline@gmail.com.
